AI mark — sparkle on the sanctioned cyan→blue→navy sheen. The ONE place the AI gradient is used. Use it on assistant chrome, the Copilot FAB, voice orb, and AI card headers.

```jsx
<AIAvatar size={36} />          // full sheen — assistant identity
<AIAvatar size={26} soft />     // tinted, low-emphasis — inside cards
```

- Never recolor it or apply the gradient elsewhere. `soft` for use on white card headers; full for the assistant's own surface.
